How to Play

Controls

  • Left Arrow (←) - Move piece left
  • Right Arrow (→) - Move piece right
  • Down Arrow (↓) - Soft drop (move down faster)
  • Up Arrow (↑) - Rotate piece
  • Space - Hard drop (instant drop)

Objective

Clear lines by completing horizontal rows with blocks. The more lines you clear at once, the more points you earn. The game ends when blocks stack to the top of the board.

Scoring

  • 1 line cleared: 100 points
  • 2 lines cleared: 300 points
  • 3 lines cleared: 500 points
  • 4 lines cleared (Tetris): 800 points
  • Soft drop: 1 point per cell
  • Hard drop: 2 points per cell

Tips

  • Try to create 4-line clears (Tetris) for maximum points
  • Plan your moves ahead to avoid getting stuck
  • Build up the sides to create wells for I-pieces
  • Keep the board as flat as possible

Game Mechanics

  • 7 different piece types (Tetriminos)
  • Difficulty increases with each level
  • Game speeds up as you clear lines
  • Preview of next piece to plan ahead
